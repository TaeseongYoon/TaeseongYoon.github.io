Hi! I am a Ph.D. candidate at KAIST, advised by Professor Heeyoung Kim at the Engineering Data Science Lab. My research primarily focuses on uncertainty quantification (UQ) for deep learning — developing scalable, theoretically grounded frameworks for reliable and trustworthy predictions, with a core emphasis on evidential deep learning, efficient Bayesian inference, out-of-distribution detection, and model robustness under distribution shifts.

Beyond foundational UQ, I am interested in statistical machine learning for complex structured and industrial data, including multivariate time-series anomaly detection, class-imbalanced semi-supervised learning, and continuous causal effect estimation. My recent work includes Courtroom Analogy (ICML 2026), F‑EDL (NeurIPS 2025), and DAEDL (ICML 2024).

Previously, I was a research intern at Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology (SAIT) and SK Hynix, where I worked on uncertainty-aware anomaly detection and quality inspection for semiconductor manufacturing.

I am happy to discuss research and collaborations. Feel free to reach out by email.
