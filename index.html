Taeseong Yoon

Ph.D. Candidate in Industrial & Systems Engineering, KAIST

I am a Ph.D. candidate at KAIST, advised by Professor Heeyoung Kim in the Engineering Data Science Lab. My research focuses on uncertainty quantification (UQ) for deep learning. I develop scalable, theoretically grounded methods for reliable prediction, with an emphasis on evidential deep learning, efficient Bayesian inference, out-of-distribution detection, and robustness under distribution shift.

Beyond UQ, I work on statistical machine learning for structured and industrial data: multivariate time-series anomaly detection, learning under incomplete and imbalanced data, causal inference, and tabular foundation models.

Open to research discussions and collaborations. Feel free to reach out.

Research

My work develops reliable and scalable uncertainty methods for deep learning, together with statistical learning methods for structured data, causal questions, and distribution shift.

Uncertainty quantification

Evidential deep learning, efficient Bayesian inference, out-of-distribution detection, and robustness under distribution shift.

Time-series and tabular learning

Multivariate time-series anomaly detection, tabular foundation models, and statistical learning for industrial data.

Causal and robust learning

Causal inference and learning under class imbalance, incomplete data, and limited supervision.
